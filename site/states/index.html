The states

One page per state, each quoting the state's own published materials on involuntary nursing-home transfer and discharge: the grounds, the notice, the office that hears the appeal, what the state publishes about bed-hold and return, and the long-term-care ombudsman's published contact. The federal floor — 42 CFR 483.15, the fair-hearing rules, and CMS guidance — is on a page of its own.

GeorgiaFour bases at Rule 111-8-50-.11(2), the emergency case stated separately; written notice at least 30 days, and 15 days for a move within the same facility; the hearing request goes in writing to the Department of Community Health, which publishes no address, email or form for it, and the state ombudsman is reached at 1-866-552-4464Aug 30, 2026

KentuckySix grounds at 900 KAR 2:050, Section 2(1), reaching relocations inside the building; notice at least 30 days; the resident informs the Cabinet for Health and Family Services in writing within 15 days, with no office, address, email or form for that filing stated in the sources reviewed; the Cabinet publishes the ombudsman by county rather than a statewide numberAug 30, 2026
