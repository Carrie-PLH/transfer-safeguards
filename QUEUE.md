# transfer-safeguards — jurisdiction queue

Build intent, not status; coverage truth lives in STATUS.md (to be
generated by tools/build-status.py once adapted). Work follows the
charter's Build order.

**Exemplars first (charter step 1):** the federal layer page (federal.md /
site/federal.html, from 42 CFR 483.15(c), 42 CFR part 431 subpart E, and
CMS's own guidance), then two states bounding the format — one hard shape
(hearing office, bed-hold publisher, and ombudsman in distinct agencies)
and one simple shape (a single agency publishes most of it). Settle the
candidates from the sources at build time.

**Full-page candidates next (charter step 2):** order by nursing-home
resident population — candidates to confirm against current federal data
at build time rather than assumed here.

**Baseline sprint order, once the gate passes:** larger resident
populations first, then the rest, adjusted freely when a reader or
distributor gives a reason to jump a state up. Populate this list when the
exemplars are done.
