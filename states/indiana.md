# Indiana

## Docket

**Grounds stated in the sources.** 410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12(a)(4): "Health facilities must permit each resident to remain in the facility and not transfer or discharge the resident from the facility unless:" — "(A) the transfer or discharge is necessary for the resident's welfare and the resident's needs cannot be met in the facility;"; "(B) the transfer or discharge is appropriate because the resident's health has improved sufficiently so that the resident no longer needs the services provided by the facility;"; "(C) the safety of individuals in the facility is endangered;"; "(D) the health of individuals in the facility would otherwise be endangered;"; "(E) the resident has failed, after reasonable and appropriate notice, to pay for (or to have paid under Medicare or Medicaid) a stay at the facility; or"; "(F) the facility ceases to operate." The department's own notice form carries the same six as checkboxes under the heading "Reason for Transfer or Discharge (Must select one of the reasons below.)".

**Notice stated in the sources.** The rule states the notice "must be made by the facility at least thirty (30) days before the resident is transferred or discharged", with five stated circumstances in which "Notice may be made as soon as practicable before transfer or discharge". The notice goes out "on a form prescribed by the department" and must reach, among others, "The local long term care ombudsman program (for involuntary relocations or discharges only)." Its required contents include "A statement in not smaller than 12-point bold type" of the appeal right, and the form requires the facility to "attach a copy of the facility’s bed hold policy to this Notice of Transfer or Discharge and provide" contact information for a facility employee about it.

**Hearing office in these sources.** The Indiana Department of Health. The prescribed notice states: "you may file a written request for a hearing with the" "Indiana State Department of Health postmarked within ten (10) days after you receive this" "notice. If you request a hearing, it will be held within twenty-three (23) days after you receive" "this notice, and you will not be transferred from the facility earlier than thirty-four (34) days" "after you receive this notice of transfer or discharge". The hearing-request form directs the resident to send it "along with the Notice of Transfer or Discharge (State Form 49669) to the Department of Health within" "ten (10) days" to "Indiana Department of Health" "Court Administrator, Office of Legal Affairs" "2 North Meridian Street – Section 3-H" "Indianapolis, Indiana 46204" or to "CourtAdministrator@health.in.gov". The notice form gives the department's questions line as "317-233-7540".

**Ombudsman in these sources.** The Office of the Long Term Care Ombudsman, which publishes its own contact as "Office of the Long Term Care Ombudsman" "402 West Washington Street, Room W451" "Post Office Box 7083, MS 27" "Indianapolis, Indiana 46207-7083", with "Information/complaint line: 800-622-4484 or 317-232-7134", "Fax number: 317-972-3285" and "Email: LongTermCareOmbudsman@ombudsman.IN.gov". The department's notice form publishes a different address for the same office, under the heading "State Long Term Care (LTC) Ombudsman", naming the "Family and Social Services Administration" at "P.O. Box 7083, 402 W. Washington St." and "IGC South, Room W451 – MS 27"; both are shown here as published.

## Lede

This page assembles what Indiana's own publishers state about involuntary transfer and discharge from a health facility. The fullest single source is 410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12, "Transfer and discharge rights", read with the two state forms the rule prescribes — the "NOTICE OF TRANSFER OR DISCHARGE", State Form 49669, and the "NOTICE OF TRANSFER OR DISCHARGE REQUEST FOR HEARING", State Form 49831 — and the Indiana Health Coverage Programs' own Provider Reference Module for the Medicaid bed-hold question the rule leaves to the state plan. Indiana's vocabulary distinguishes an "interfacility transfer and discharge", defined as "the movement of a resident to a bed outside of the" "licensed facility", from an "intrafacility transfer" within the same facility — this page covers only the former, per this project's scope; the appeal is a written request for a hearing to the Department of Health. Three publishers hold it: the Department of Health writes the rule, prescribes both forms and hears the appeal; the Office of the Long Term Care Ombudsman sits separately; and the state Medicaid program, administered through the Indiana Health Coverage Programs (IHCP), publishes the bed-hold payment policy the rule itself does not state a duration for.

The federal floor for Medicare/Medicaid-certified facilities is assembled separately on [the federal page](../federal.html); nothing here fills a gap from federal law or another state. Confirm applicability with the agencies named in the sources: whether any provision applies to a particular facility, resident, or notice is for the hearing office to determine. Deadlines and periods on this page are quoted from the sources, not calculated for any case.

## 01 — The grounds, as Indiana describes them

410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12(a)(4), "Transfer and discharge rights" ([Indiana Department of Health (PDF)](https://www.in.gov/health/files/A00162.pdf), rule history line ends readopted filed Nov 13, 2019, retrieved 2026-09-13), states that health facilities "must permit each resident to remain in the facility and not transfer or discharge the resident from the facility unless", in the rule's own order:

"the transfer or discharge is necessary for the resident's welfare and the resident's needs cannot be met in the facility;"

"the transfer or discharge is appropriate because the resident's health has improved sufficiently so that the resident no longer needs the services provided by the facility;"

"the safety of individuals in the facility is endangered;"

"the health of individuals in the facility would otherwise be endangered;"

"the resident has failed, after reasonable and appropriate notice, to pay for (or to have paid under Medicare or Medicaid) a stay at the facility; or"

"the facility ceases to operate."

The department's own prescribed notice, State Form 49669 (R8 / 1-19), restates the same six bases as checkboxes under the heading "Reason for Transfer or Discharge (Must select one of the reasons below.)", including "The transfer or discharge is necessary to meet the resident’s welfare and the resident’s needs cannot be met in the facility." — the form's own wording differs slightly from the rule's ("necessary to meet the resident's welfare" against "necessary for the resident's welfare"); both stand as published.

## 02 — The published process

### Requesting the hearing

410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12(a)(12): "The department shall provide a resident who wishes to appeal the transfer or discharge from a facility the opportunity to file a request for a hearing postmarked within ten (10) days following the resident's receipt of the written notice of the transfer or discharge from the facility." Subdivision (13): "If a facility resident requests a hearing, the department shall hold an informal hearing at the facility within twenty-three (23) days from the date the resident receives the notice of transfer or discharge." "The facility must convince the department by a preponderance of the" "evidence that the transfer or discharge is authorized under subdivision (4)." The pendency statement, subdivision (10): "If the resident appeals the transfer or discharge, the facility may not transfer or discharge the resident within thirty-four" "(34) days after the resident receives the initial transfer or discharge notice, unless an emergency exists as provided under" "subdivision (8)."

The hearing-request form, State Form 49831 (R8 / 4-23), directs the resident to send it "along with the Notice of Transfer or Discharge (State Form 49669) to the Department of Health within" "ten (10) days of your receiving the notice of transfer or discharge from the facility" to "Indiana Department of Health" "Court Administrator, Office of Legal Affairs" "2 North Meridian Street – Section 3-H" "Indianapolis, Indiana 46204", "Or" by email to "CourtAdministrator@health.in.gov". No filing fee or filing form other than State Form 49831 is stated in the sources reviewed.

### The notice's required contents

410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12(a)(9): "For health facilities, the written notice specified in subdivision (7) must include the following:" — "(A) The reason for transfer or discharge."; "(B) The effective date of transfer or discharge."; "(C) The location to which the resident is transferred or discharged."; a statement "in not smaller than 12-point bold type that reads," the resident's appeal right in full, quoted in the docket above; "(E) The name of the director, address, telephone number, and hours of operation of the division."; "(F) A hearing request form prescribed by the department."; "(G) The name, address, and telephone number of the division and local long term care ombudsman."; and, "(H) For facility residents with developmental disabilities or who are mentally ill, the mailing address and telephone" "number of the protection and advocacy services commission."

The form the resident actually receives, State Form 49669, carries the same appeal-rights paragraph with its own citation form: "unless" "the facility is authorized to transfer" "you as an emergency transfer under 410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12(a)8." and directs questions to "the Indiana State Department of Health at 317-233-7540" "between the hours of 8:15 am and 4:45 pm." The form also requires the facility to attach "the facility’s bed hold policy to this Notice of Transfer or Discharge and provide" the bed-hold contact, and to include, for residents "who are mentally ill or" "developmentally disabled", the contact for "Indiana Disability Rights" at "4701 North Keystone Avenue, Suite 222" "Indianapolis, IN 46205", "Voice 1-800/622-4845 or 317/722-5555", "TTY 1-800/838-1131; Fax 317/722-5564".

### Bed-hold and return

410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12(a)(25): "Before a facility transfers a resident to a hospital or allows a resident to go on therapeutic leave of twenty-four" "(24) hours duration or longer, the facility must provide written information to the resident and a family member or legal representative" "that specifies the following:" "(A) The duration of the bed-hold policy under the Medicaid state plan during which the resident is permitted to return" "and resume residence in the facility." and "(B) The facility's policies regarding bed-hold periods, which must be consistent with subdivision (27), permitting a" "resident to return." Subdivision (26): "Except in an emergency, at the time of transfer of a resident for hospitalization or therapeutic leave," a facility must provide "written notice which specifies the duration of the bed-hold policy" "described in subdivision (25)." Subdivision (27): "Medicaid certified facilities must establish and follow a written policy under which a resident, whose hospitalization or" "therapeutic leave exceeds the bed-hold period under the state plan, is readmitted to the facility immediately upon the first" "availability of a bed in a semiprivate room if the resident:" "requires the services provided by the facility; and" "is eligible for Medicaid nursing facility services."

The rule's own state-plan cross-reference is answered by the Indiana Health Coverage Programs' Provider Reference Module for long-term care facility services (Library Reference Number PROMOD00037, published May 12, 2026, policies and procedures as of Aug. 1, 2025), under the heading "Leave Days in a Nursing Facility": "The IHCP does not reimburse for bed-hold days in an NF as a member benefit unless the member is under" "the care of hospice." "There is no requirement that NFs hold beds." "An NF is required to follow a written policy under which a resident, whose hospital or" "therapeutic leave exceeds Medicaid coverage limitations, is readmitted to the facility upon the first" "availability of a bed in a semiprivate room, if the resident requires NF-level services and is eligible for" "Medicaid NF services." "Because Medicaid does not pay to hold beds in NFs except for hospice care, all bed holds for days of" "absence are considered noncovered services for which the resident may elect to pay." The module states plainly that a facility "must inform a resident in writing prior to a hospital transfer or departure for therapeutic leave" "that Medicaid does not pay for bed holds" and must "also communicate its policies regarding bed-" "hold periods." The two publishers are shown together rather than reconciled: the rule directs a facility to disclose "The duration of the bed-hold policy under the Medicaid state plan" as a state-plan matter, and the state's own Medicaid module states that no such paid duration exists outside hospice care — the disclosed duration, where one exists at all, is the facility's own private bed-hold policy, not a state-plan payment period.

### Notice periods and deadlines, as stated in the sources

All quoted; none calculated.

Thirty days' advance notice — 410 IAC 16.2-3.1-12(a)(7): the notice of transfer or discharge "must be made" "by the facility at least thirty (30) days before the resident is transferred or discharged."

As-soon-as-practicable exceptions — subdivision (8): "Notice may be made as soon as practicable before transfer or discharge when:" the safety or health of individuals in the facility would be endangered, the resident's health improves sufficiently to allow a more immediate transfer or discharge, an immediate transfer or discharge is required by the resident's urgent medical needs, or "a resident has not resided in the facility for thirty (30) days."

Ten days to request a hearing — subdivision (12), quoted above in full; State Form 49831 states the same period as "within" "ten (10) days of your receiving the notice of transfer or discharge from the facility".

Twenty-three days to the informal hearing — subdivision (13): the department "shall hold an informal hearing at the facility within twenty-three" "(23) days from the date the resident receives the notice of transfer or discharge". Subdivision (13) also states the department "shall attempt to give at least" "five (5) days written notice to all parties prior to the informal hearing."

Thirty-four days before transfer if the resident appeals — subdivision (10), quoted above in full; the same figure appears in the notice's own appeal-rights paragraph.

Thirty days for the department's decision — subdivision (13): the department "shall issue a decision within thirty" "(30) days from the date the resident receives the notice."

Twenty-four hours of therapeutic leave triggering the bed-hold disclosure — subdivision (25): "Before a facility transfers a resident to a hospital or allows a resident to go on therapeutic leave of twenty-four" "(24) hours duration or longer, the facility must provide written information" to the resident and family member or legal representative specifying the bed-hold policy, quoted above in full.

What any of these periods means for a particular case is for the hearing office to determine.
